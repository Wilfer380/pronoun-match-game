01 · PURPOSE

Grammar becomes easier in real situations

Objective

To practice personal, possessive, and object pronouns with short sentences from school, home, family, and friends.

Objetivo

Practicar los pronombres personales, posesivos y de objeto con oraciones cortas sobre la escuela, la casa, la familia y los amigos.

02 · QUICK GUIDE

Three types, three simple jobs

Personal pronouns

They replace the person who does the action.
She is my sister. · We study.

I · you · he · she · we · they

Possessives

They show who owns something.
This is my bag. · The pencil is hers.

my · your · his · hers · ours

Object pronouns

They receive the action.
Help me. · I call him.

me · him · her · us · them

03 · HOW TO PLAY

Find the sentence and its correct pronoun

English

  1. Put all the cards face down.
  2. Flip two cards per turn.
  3. Match a sentence with the correct pronoun.
  4. If they match, keep the pair.
  5. If not, the cards flip back.
  6. Follow the wildcards.
  7. The player with the most pairs wins.

Español

  1. Coloca las tarjetas boca abajo.
  2. Voltea dos tarjetas por turno.
  3. Une una oración con el pronombre correcto.
  4. Si coinciden, conserva la pareja.
  5. Si no, las tarjetas se voltean.
  6. Sigue las instrucciones de los comodines.
  7. Gana quien tenga más parejas.
⭐ Extra Turn = play again   ·   ↩ Lose a Pair = return one pair

05 · EXAMPLES

See how each pronoun works

Laura is my sister. She is kind.

EN: “She” replaces the name Laura.
ES: “She” reemplaza el nombre Laura.

This is her bag.

EN: “Her” shows who owns the bag.
ES: “Her” indica quién es la dueña del bolso.

I like him.

EN: “Him” receives the action.
ES: “Him” recibe la acción.

That book is mine.

EN: “Mine” replaces the object and shows possession.
ES: “Mine” reemplaza el objeto y expresa posesión.

06 · GUIDING QUESTIONS

Pronouns make daily messages clear

Why is it important to use pronouns correctly?

EN: Pronouns help me speak clearly and avoid repeating names. They make my ideas shorter and easier to understand.

ES: Los pronombres me ayudan a hablar con claridad y a evitar repetir nombres.

How does the game make grammar fun?

EN: The game helps me read, remember, and choose the correct pronoun. Colors and daily examples make grammar more fun.

ES: El juego me ayuda a leer, recordar y elegir el pronombre correcto.

07 · METACOGNITION

What I learned while creating and playing

Creating and playing this memory game helped me understand pronouns in a simple and fun way. The sentences are about school, home, family, and friends, so I can connect grammar with my daily life.

Personal pronouns were easier for me because I often use I, he, she, and we. Object pronouns were a little more difficult because I sometimes confuse he with him or we with us.

The colors helped me identify each type of pronoun. Blue was for personal pronouns, green for possessives, and orange for object pronouns. I also used repetition and short examples to avoid mistakes.

Pronouns are useful when I introduce myself, talk about my family and friends, or describe my things. The cards, colors, and wildcards made the activity more creative. The game showed me that grammar can be practical and enjoyable.

Traducción: Crear y jugar este juego de memoria me ayudó a comprender los pronombres de una manera sencilla y divertida. Los ejemplos cotidianos y los colores facilitaron mi aprendizaje. Los pronombres de objeto fueron un poco más difíciles, pero la repetición me ayudó a evitar errores.

Conclusion / Conclusión: This game helped me practice pronouns with real situations in a fun way. / Este juego me ayudó a practicar los pronombres con situaciones reales de una forma divertida.
